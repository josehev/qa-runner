## 1. ACCESSIBILITY (WCAG 2.1/2.0 AA)

**Keyboard navigation — PARTIAL FAIL**
- Tabbed through first 15 focusable elements and diffed computed `outline`/`box-shadow`/`border` before vs. after `.focus()`.
- PASS: "Skip to Main Content", "Home" logo link, "Rebates, Incentives & Credits" breadcrumb, "About" accordion button, "BACK TO TOP" link — all show `outline-style: solid` on focus.
- **FAIL**: "Contact Us" header link and all top-nav buttons ("Account & Billing", "Services & Outages", "Save Energy & Money", "Clean Energy", "Search", "Log in or Register") have `outline-style: none` in both focused and unfocused states — confirmed via direct computed-style diff (outline stayed `"none"`, only `outline-width` changed from 3px→0px, which is irrelevant since style is none; no background/border/box-shadow change either). **Result: these elements have no visible focus indicator at all for keyboard users.**
  - Repro: Load page → press Tab repeatedly to reach "Contact Us" or any top nav button → observe no visible focus ring.
- **FAIL**: Pressing `Escape` while the "Log in or Register" dropdown/panel is open does not close it (`aria-expanded` stayed `"true"`). Clicking outside the panel also does not close it. Only re-clicking the toggle button closes it.
  - Repro: Click "Log in or Register" → press Escape → panel remains open.

**Screen reader compatibility / landmarks — FAIL**

- **`<main>` element: 0, `role="main"`: 0** — confirmed via raw HTML grep, no `<main>` tag or `role="main"` anywhere on the page. The "Skip to Main Content" link points to `id="mainContent"` on a plain `<div>`, not a semantic landmark.
