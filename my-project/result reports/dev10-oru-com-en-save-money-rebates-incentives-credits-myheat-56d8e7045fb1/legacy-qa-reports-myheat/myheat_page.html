    Get a Free Home Energy Profile

      Get a free personalized Home Energy Profile to see where your home may be losing heat, improve comfort, and explore ways to save energy and lower upgrade costs.

      About

      About

       

      Rockland Electric has partnered with MyHEAT to help homeowners identify and reduce heat loss through aerial thermal infrared imaging. This technology uses specialized cameras on aircraft to capture heat signatures from above, creating a map that shows where your home may be losing heat and how it compares with similar homes in your area. Based on your results, you’ll receive personalized recommendations for energy-saving improvements, along with rebates and incentives that may help lower upgrade costs.

      Benefits of MyHEAT

      Understand Your Home’s Heat Loss

      Get a free, personalized view of where your home may be losing heat and how well it retains energy.

      Compare Your Home’s Performance

      Reduce Heat Loss and Improve Comfort

      Get Started

      Step 1: Set Up Your Profile

      Visit MyHEAT’s website, enter your address and the first four digits of your Rockland Electric account number to access your heat loss profile.

      VISIT MYHEAT

      Step 2: Review Your Results

      Review your home’s heat loss score, identify potential areas of heat loss, and compare your results with similar homes nearby.

      Step 3: Explore Ways to Save

      Receive personalized energy-saving recommendations based on your home’s results and discover rebates, incentives, and programs that can help lower upgrade costs. You can also get a no-cost Home Energy Assessment for a more in-depth look at ways to improve your home’s energy efficiency.

      FAQs

      A: Your MyHEAT score compares your home’s heat loss with similar homes of the same size and age in your area. A low score means your home retains heat well, while a high score means your home may be losing more heat and could benefit from energy-efficient improvements.

      A: Thermal data was collected using a high-precision sensor in December 2024 for homes within the program area. The data was captured by an aircraft flying at night when outdoor temperatures were near freezing, ensuring optimal conditions for accurate thermal imaging. This process is similar to how mapping platforms like Google Maps gather aerial imagery. To protect privacy, MyHEAT’s sensor cannot see inside homes or through structures.

      After collection, the thermal data is processed using advanced machine learning algorithms to identify areas of heat loss from a bird’s-eye view. The analysis produces unique heat loss ratings and detailed heat maps for individual buildings, which are shared with customers through MyHEAT’s educational platform.

      A: Thermal data was collected using a high-precision sensor in December 2024 for homes within the program area. Any home improvements made after that will not appear in your results until MyHEAT updates its imaging for this program.
      A: If MyHEAT identifies areas of heat loss, you can review the recommended energy-efficient improvements in your home energy profile and explore available Rockland Electric programs, rebates, and incentives that may help you make upgrades. This information is provided for informational purposes, and you are not required to complete any actions suggested by the platform. However, we encourage you to learn more about available rebate programs and consider scheduling a no-cost Home Energy Assessment or Home Energy Assessment Plus to identify additional opportunities to improve your home’s energy efficiency.
